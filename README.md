# Album 42 - New Jerusalem

A powerful multilingual Christian worship album focused on the New Jerusalem descending from heaven (Revelation 21). The songs celebrate the holy city where the Lamb is the temple, the end of all earthly matrices and empires, and the eternal victory and oneness of God. Features rich styles including Georgian chant, Italian classical, Arabic, Japanese, and global worship.
